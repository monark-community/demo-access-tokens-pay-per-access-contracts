# Assets

All photographs are from Unsplash under the free [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license); none are Unsplash+ (every download redirected to `images.unsplash.com`). Each is stored in `public/images/` at 2000px on its long edge (portraits 800×800), JPEG quality ~72, progressive, metadata stripped, and served with `next/image` from a static import. Photographers are credited on `/credits` (linked from the footer) from `src/lib/photos.ts`, which this table mirrors.

The "digital keys" revision (2026-09-30) added 20 photos for the city courts, the gate carousels (three per gate), the owners' portraits and `/how-it-works`, and replaced two warm originals (an ON AIR sign and an orange-lit stage) with cool versions of the same subjects.
